    - Procedural sound generators:
      1. `startAmbientDrone()` & `stopAmbientDrone()`:
         - Dual detuned oscillators (55Hz sub-bass and 110Hz harmonic) through a 24dB low-pass filter modulated by a slow 0.12Hz LFO, faded in over 1.2s.
         - Graceful fade-out over 0.8s on stop.

         - High-frequency tactical chirp (1200Hz to 600Hz exponential ramp, 40ms decay).

         - Mechanical keystroke click (1800Hz damped pulse, 14ms duration).
      5. `playModalOpen()`:
         - Ascending HUD frequency sweep (400Hz to 1600Hz, bandpass filtered, 100ms).
      6. `playAccessDenied()`:
         - Tactical low-frequency double pulse (180Hz square waves, 75ms).
    - Export `soundService` singleton.
  </action>
